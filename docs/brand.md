# MyToolMint brand guide

Status: Approved — Option B, “The Tool Spark”

## Brand idea

The dark tile communicates confidence and reliability. The mint geometric M keeps the mark friendly and recognizable, while the small white spark represents a quick, useful answer.

## Core colours

- MyToolMint Navy: `#081A22`
- MyToolMint Mint: `#49D5AC`
- Primary interface mint: `#19B88A`
- Soft mint: `#DFF8EF`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Typography

Use Inter ExtraBold for the wordmark where Inter is available. The website uses its system-sans fallback stack to avoid an external font request and protect performance.

## Usage

- Use `logo-horizontal.svg` on light backgrounds.
- Use `logo-horizontal-reversed.svg` on navy or dark backgrounds.
- Use `logo-mark.svg` for favicons, social avatars and compact placements.
- Use `logo-lockup-tagline.svg` when the positioning line is helpful and space allows.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one quarter of the mark’s width.
- Do not rotate, stretch, recolour, outline or rearrange the mark.

The logo remains readable down to 32 pixels. Below that size, use the mark without the wordmark.
